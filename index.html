    Questions, answered.

    The things people ask before they install a money app. Short answers, no fine print.

    Does Penny connect to my bank?

    No. Penny never asks for a bank password and never logs in anywhere. You download a statement (CSV, OFX or QFX) from your bank's website and drop it in. Major US banks are recognized automatically.

    Where is my data, and who can see it?

    Only on your PC, encrypted with AES-256 and tied to your Windows account. There is no account and no cloud, and Penny has no servers. Add a passcode for a second lock, with a recovery key in case you forget it.

    Is it really free?

    Yes, everything is free during the beta. A Pro plan will add extras later; everyday budgeting stays free, and data you already have is never removed or locked away.

    How is the health score worked out?

    Four pillars (Spend, Save, Borrow, Plan) scored 0 to 100 with the Penny Research model, built from the latest household-finance research and tested on realistic households. Read the method.

    What if I already use Mint, YNAB or Monarch?

    Export from your old app and import the file. Penny recognizes those exports with all their accounts, keeps transfers as transfers and skips duplicates.

    Mac, Linux or phone?

    Penny is for Windows 10 and 11 today. The money engine is already shared code, which is the groundwork for a phone version. Join the Discord to hear first.
