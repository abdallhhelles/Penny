# Penny Beta 1.0.3

- **Desktop widget.** A small window with what's safe to spend today, your budgets, what's coming up and a quick-add box: type "coffee 4.50 at Starbucks", press Enter, done. Drag it anywhere (it remembers where), pin it on top, or close it. Turn it on in Settings › Windows, from the tray menu, or from Penny's taskbar jump list. It respects privacy mode and shows nothing while Penny is locked.
- **Achievements and streaks.** 24 badges for good money habits (first budget, a week-long streak, a month within budget, a 3-month safety net and more), a streak that counts the days you look after your money, and an "I spent nothing today" button. On a new Achievements page and as a card on the Overview. Private: nothing is shared or compared.
- **Penny remembers your choices.** Import a bank file once and next time the same bank's file goes to the same account with the same columns and date format. New spending, income and transfers each start on the account you last used for them. Your sort order, Insights range and Plan sliders come back the way you left them, and file dialogs open in the folder you used last.
- **Works at every window size.** Narrow windows get a proper bottom tab bar (Overview, Transactions, Budgets, Insights and More) instead of 12 tiny icons; it was squashed at the top of the window before. Medium windows keep small cards two across, and long words in Spanish, Chinese and other languages no longer push pages sideways.
- **Calmer, more consistent design.** Quieter reminder tiles, a cleaner Overview hero, and spacing that lines up across pages.
