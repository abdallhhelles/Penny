# Penny Beta 1.0.1

- **A tour that fits you.** New users pick what they want help with (spend less, save for something, pay off debt, never miss a bill). The tour shows only what matters for that, lets you try smart entry right inside the tour, and ends with matching next steps. Tips follow your choice too.
- **Smarter Ask Penny.** Ask "how much can I spend today?", "how much budget is left?", "which bills are due?" or "how is my Japan trip goal doing?", in English, Chinese, Hindi, Spanish or Arabic.
- **Payee memory.** Type a payee you have used before and Penny shows what it usually costs and where it goes; one tap fills it in.
- **No more notification spam.** Pressing a button repeatedly shows one message with a counter instead of a stack, a quick double-click can never save something twice, and Windows notifications are never repeated or sent in bursts.
- **Cleaner, more even layout.** Equal margins on both sides of every page, no half-empty rows on the Overview, three-across rows stay three across, and compact account cards.
- Fixes: currency codes such as "US$" no longer appear reversed in Arabic; new transactions default to "Other" correctly in every language.
- **Ready for the Microsoft Store.** Penny can now be built as a Store package; in the Store version, Windows handles updates and startup, notifications keep working, and "Open data folder" opens the right place.
- The publisher is now shown as **Akrot Software** (license agreement and privacy notice updated to match).
