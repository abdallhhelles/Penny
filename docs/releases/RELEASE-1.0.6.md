# Penny Beta 1.0.6

- **Start with your real numbers.** After the welcome screen, Penny now asks for a statement from your bank or card first: drop the file in, or choose it. Prefer typing? "coffee 4.50 at Starbucks" works too, or look around with sample data.
- **Drop a statement anywhere.** Drag a CSV, OFX or QFX file from your Downloads onto Penny's window, any time, and the import starts.
- **See what Penny found.** Your first import ends with a short summary: how many transactions, the dates they cover, what went out and where, and the repeating charges Penny spotted. One question (today's balance) makes "safe to spend today" right from day one.
- **No more empty dashboard.** Until you have data, the overview shows one simple way in instead of a page of zeros.
- Fixed: a card payment in a brand-new setup was counted as income.
- Fixed: payee names with an apostrophe ("Trader Joe's") are no longer capitalized as "Joe'S".
