# Penny Beta 1.0.4

- **Your bank's statements, read for you.** Files from Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Capital One, Citi, American Express, Discover, Apple Card, U.S. Bank and Ally are recognized automatically: Penny picks the right account, sets the columns and dates, and gets the signs right (card files that list purchases as positive are flipped for you). Bank of America's summary lines above the header and Wells Fargo's header-less files just work.
- **Coming from another app?** Mint, YNAB and Monarch exports are recognized too, with all their accounts: choose where each one goes or let Penny create it. Transfers between your accounts are kept as transfers (YNAB) or left out (Mint, Monarch) so nothing is counted twice.
- **Clean payee names.** "SQ *BLUE BOTTLE COFFEE 0123 OAKLAND CA" becomes "Blue Bottle Coffee", "AMZN Mktp US*2K4L19ZQ0" becomes "Amazon", and the original text is kept in the note. Bank categories ("Food & Drink", "Supermarkets", "Gasoline") map onto Penny's, and well-known US stores land in the right category.
- **Card payments are transfers, not spending.** Paying your card from checking is recorded once, as a transfer, whichever statement you import first, so your budgets and income stay right.
- **"Where do I get this file?"** A short guide in the import window.
- **Rate Penny.** In the Microsoft Store version, Penny may ask "Enjoying Penny?" once in a while, only after a week of real use and right after something good (a new badge, a goal reached). Yes takes you to the Store's review page; no asks what to fix instead. At most three times, never again after you rate. Settings › About also has "Rate Penny" and a new "Send feedback".
- Amounts typed in one line ("coffee 4.50 …") now show as 4.50, not 4.5.
