# Penny Beta 1.0.5

- **Privacy mode now hides everything.** Amounts, percentages, scores and chart labels are replaced with •••, instead of being blurred. Chart tooltips and axes show no numbers either, and nothing reappears when you hover. Press P (or the eye at the top) to turn it on and off.
- **Export to Excel.** Transactions › Excel (and Settings › Data) saves a real .xlsx file: proper dates and amounts you can sort and sum, plus a sheet of totals by category.
- **Accent colours.** Settings › Appearance lets you pick Penny's accent colour: copper, teal, indigo, plum, rose or ocean, in light and dark.
- **Penny Pro, coming soon.** Settings has a new Penny Pro section showing what Pro will add. Everything stays free during the beta.
- Under the hood: Penny's money engine now lives in one shared core, the groundwork for Penny on your phone. Your data and how it's stored are unchanged.
